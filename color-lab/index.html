Privacy Policy for Color Lab

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Color Lab: Kids Painting ("Color Lab") is a painting game for children, published by Faizan Humayun. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: the app does not collect any information about you or your child.

Information we collect

None. Color Lab does not collect, store or share any personal information. We do not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number, a photo, a voice recording, a location or an account. There is no sign-in and no way to create a profile.

Internet access

The app does not use the internet. It does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your device to us or to anyone else. The game works completely offline.

Spoken words

Color Lab can say short phrases out loud, such as color names. It does this with the text-to-speech feature already built into the phone or tablet. The app only speaks; it never listens, and it does not use the microphone. The phrases are fixed parts of the game and contain no information about your child. The voice can be switched off in the app's Settings.

Advertising and analytics

The app shows no advertisements. It contains no advertising networks, no analytics tools, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. Nothing about how your child plays is measured or reported.

What is saved on the device

The app saves a few small settings on the phone or tablet itself, so the game can continue where your child left off:

  • the highest level reached, and the stars earned on each level
  • whether sound effects, the voice and vibration are on or off
  • which avatar was chosen

This information stays on the device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Android backup

Android may copy those same settings into the device owner's own Google account as part of the phone's standard backup feature, so that progress returns if the phone is replaced. This is handled entirely by Android and Google, not by us, and we have no access to it. The phone's owner can turn backup off in the Android settings.

Children's privacy

Color Lab is designed for children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent to review, change or delete on our side. This approach is intended to meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases and no payments of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
